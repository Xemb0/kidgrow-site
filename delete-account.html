Delete your account or data

A parent can remove a child’s data or the entire KidGrow account.

Updated September 22, 2026

Delete in KidGrow

  1. Open Settings and verify grown-up access.
  2. Choose Profile, then Account & privacy.
  3. Tap Delete parent account and confirm. An internet connection is required.

This removes your parent account and associated child profiles and cloud learning data. Other devices must reconnect to receive changes. Locally saved Moments are not part of cloud progress backup.

Delete only a child or learning history

In the same Profile section, choose Clear dated history, Reset learning and history, or Start over with this child. Read the confirmation to see what will be erased. You do not need to delete the parent account to clear a child’s history.

Request deletion without the app

Email vishalnishadofficials@gmail.com with the subject “KidGrow account deletion request”. Include the parent account email and whether you want the entire account or only particular data removed. We will verify account ownership before acting. Never send your password or a child’s photo.

What may remain

Opaque deletion markers prevent erased profiles from being restored by an offline device. Provider backups, operational logs and store transaction records may follow separate recovery, security or legal retention schedules. RevenueCat purchase records are not automatically erased by in-app account deletion; include these in your support request if you want provider-held data reviewed for deletion. See the privacy policy.

Cancel billing separately

Deleting your account or uninstalling does not cancel a subscription. Manage an Apple subscription in device Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Manage Google Play subscriptions in Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
